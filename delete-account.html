Kanji Game — Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in Kanji Game (formerly Kanji Tour), the GyanMirai kanji game (Kanji Game: Japan Tour on Google Play, and its web version), published by GyanMirai. Signing in is optional; if you never signed in to the Game, we hold no account or progress data from it and there is nothing to delete here. See also the Kanji Game Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the Game

  1. Open Kanji Game and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Me tab.
  3. In the Account card, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything synced to it are deleted from our servers immediately, and you are signed out on that device. The Game signs you in with your GyanMirai account, the same account you use on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts, so deleting it deletes it everywhere: your gyanmirai.com profile, website progress and test results and the synced progress of every GyanMirai app are deleted too, and you can no longer sign in with it. The progress stored on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or use Me → Settings → Reset progress.

2. Delete your account by email

If you can't use the Game, email hello@gyanmirai.com from the email address your account uses and ask us to delete it. We delete the account and all of its synced data within 30 days and confirm by email.

While GyanMirai Pro bought on gyanmirai.com (a subscription or an exam-season pass) is still running on your account, the Game can't delete the account: cancel the subscription on gyanmirai.com and try again once your Pro has ended, or email us and we will delete it for you.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account (email address, sign-in method, user ID, and the Google profile details if you used Google) and all synced game progress, settings and statistics, plus everything else linked to your GyanMirai account.
  • Kept: nothing on our servers. Copies in our encrypted backups are removed within 30 days. Data on your own device stays until you uninstall the Game or reset it.
