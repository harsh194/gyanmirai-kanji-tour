Kanji Tour — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy covers Kanji Tour, the GyanMirai kanji game, listed on Google Play as Kanji Tour: Kanji Game, and its web version on gyanmirai.com (together the "Game"), published by GyanMirai ("we," "our," or "us"). It explains what data the Game uses, who else receives it, and the choices you have. For the rest of the gyanmirai.com website, see our website Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • The Game works fully without an account. Signing in is optional, and no part of the Game is locked behind it.
  • If you sign in, you sign in with your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts.
  • If you don't sign in, your kanji progress, cities, stamps, scores, coins and settings are stored only on your device and we do not receive them.
  • If you sign in, we store your account (email address and basic profile details) and a copy of your game progress on our servers, so it can be backed up and synced across your devices.
  • You can delete your account and its synced data in the Game (Me → Account → Delete account) or at any time by email. Deleting your GyanMirai account deletes it everywhere: on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only with your consent). Ads are never shown during a run. The web version shows no ads.
  • The Game has no in-app purchases, and apart from Google AdMob it contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your tour progress (the kanji and words you have met and how well you remember each one, the cities you have unlocked, your city stamps, levels, best scores and race times), your coins and the car parts and outfits you have collected, your daily quests and streak, your play statistics (runs played, answers, play time), the tour name you picked from the Game's list of names, and your settings (volumes, romaji, reduced motion, tilt steering) are kept in the Game's own storage on your device: the app's private storage on Android, your browser's local storage in the web version. Race "ghosts" (recordings of your own best laps) are kept there too. If you don't sign in, none of this is ever uploaded to us.

Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in Android settings (clearing site data in your browser for the web version), deletes it. On Android, the Game's data may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account, which you control in Android's backup settings and which is covered by Google's policy, not ours.

On Android the Game shows no permission prompts. It declares only permissions that Android grants without asking: internet access (for the optional account and sync, and for ads), vibration (short haptic feedback during play), the advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob (see Advertising) and the credential permissions that Android's own Google account picker uses if you choose to sign in with Google. Tilt steering reads your phone's motion sensor (how far the phone is tilted left or right) to steer while you play; these readings are used on the device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere. The Game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. Pronunciation audio and fonts ship inside the app, so playing offline sends nothing anywhere (ads need a connection; offline, none are loaded).

3. Your account (optional)

The Game can let you sign in to back up your progress and sync it between devices. No feature of the Game requires an account.

One GyanMirai account

Signing in uses your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts. If you don't have one yet, signing in creates it. You sign in with your email address and password (the same login as gyanmirai.com), with a one-time 6-digit code sent to your email address, or with Google, whichever the Game offers on your platform.

What we receive and store

  • Account details: with email and password or an email code, we receive only your email address (your password is stored only in protected, hashed form and we never see it). If you sign in with Google, Google confirms your identity and shares your email address, a Google account identifier and the basic profile details Google provides (your name and profile picture) with our sign-in service; the Game itself uses only your email address and user ID. Our backend stores your account as a user record containing your email address, the sign-in method, a random user ID we assign and, if you use Google, the Google details above.
  • gyanmirai.com profile: because the account is your GyanMirai account, it also has a gyanmirai.com profile, created automatically the first time you sign in, with your email address, your name and profile picture link (if Google provides them) and the sign-in method. What you do on gyanmirai.com is covered by our website Privacy Policy.
  • Game progress: while you are signed in, the Game keeps a copy of your progress on our servers and syncs it to your other signed-in devices: your kanji and word learning progress (which ones you have met and the review schedule for each), the cities you have unlocked, your city stamps, levels, best scores and race best times, your coins and the car parts and outfits you own, your daily quests and streak, your play statistics, your settings, and your tour name (a name chosen from the Game's list, not your real name). This is linked to your user ID.
  • Never uploaded: race ghosts (your recorded laps), the local league boards, "new" badges and your current place in the tour stay on your device. The Game also keeps a small sync journal on the device (which items changed since the last sync); it is used only to decide what to send and is not itself uploaded.

How we use it

Only to provide the account: to sign you in, back up your progress and keep it in sync across your devices, and to let you use the same account on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps. We do not use account or progress data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with Supabase, our backend provider, in its Tokyo (Japan) region, and are only accessible to your account. If you live outside Japan, this means your data is transferred to and processed in Japan. Supabase processes it on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Signing out, resetting and deleting your account

Sign out (Me → Account → Sign out) stops syncing; the progress on your device stays, and so does the copy in your account until you delete the account.

Reset progress (Me → Settings → Reset progress) starts you over. If you are signed in, it clears your kanji and word progress, coins, collected items, quests and statistics both on the device and in your account; your city stamps, unlocked cities and best scores are kept on the account. If you are not signed in, it clears everything on the device.

Delete account: open the Game, sign in with the account you want to delete, open the Me tab, then Account → Delete account and confirm. This immediately deletes your GyanMirai account everywhere (on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app), together with everything synced to it, including your gyanmirai.com profile, website progress and test results and the synced progress of every GyanMirai app. The progress on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game or reset it.

While GyanMirai Pro bought on gyanmirai.com (a subscription or an exam-season pass) is still running on your account, the Game can't delete the account: cancel the subscription on gyanmirai.com and try again once your Pro has ended, or email hello@gyanmirai.com and we will delete the account for you. You can ask us to delete your account by email at any time, from the email address the account uses; we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

4. Sign-in emails

If you choose "Email me a code", we email you a one-time 6-digit code. These emails are sent by GyanMirai through Resend, our email delivery provider, which receives your email address and the message in order to deliver it and processes them on our behalf under its privacy policy. Entering a code for an email address that has no GyanMirai account yet creates one. We send no marketing emails from the Game.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the menu screens (Play, Collection, Me and its pages), and, from your third run on, sometimes a full-screen ad when you leave the results screen after a run (at most one every second run, and never more than one every two minutes). Ads are never shown during a run, on the results screen itself or before your first run is over. The web version shows no ads. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences up to "parental guidance".

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system
  • ad impressions, clicks, diagnostic data and crash logs

Google uses this data to show, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not send Google your account, email address or game progress.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Me → Settings → Ad privacy choices in the Game.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

No purchases, no other tracking

The Game has no in-app purchases and no paid content. Apart from Google AdMob as described above, it includes no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. If this ever changes, we will update this policy before the new version is released.

7. Children

Kanji Tour is intended for teenagers and adults (13+) and is not directed at children. Its ads are limited to content rated up to "parental guidance" (see Advertising). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account or provided data through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and retention

Data sent by the Game travels over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections, and synced data can only be read by the account it belongs to: our database enforces this per row, so no account can read another's progress. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game, clear its data or reset your progress.

If you sign in, your account and synced progress are kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, it is removed from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days. Data held by Google (including AdMob) and Resend is kept according to their policies linked above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. If you have not signed in, we do not hold your game data on any server (your ad choices are in Advertising), so you can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data, resetting your progress or uninstalling it. If you have an account, you can delete it and its synced data: use Me → Account → Delete account in the Game as described above and on our account deletion page, or email us. For access, correction or anything else, email us (from your account's email address if it is about your account) and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we change how the Game handles your data, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
